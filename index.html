    How to Play

    1. Pick your riders. Choose 2–4 players and give each one a colour.
    2. Roll the dice. Tap ROLL DICE on your turn and ride that many squares.
    3. Handle the road. Markets give coins, potholes push you back, police checks cost you.
    4. Land exactly on FINISH. Overshooting means you stay put and wait for a better roll.
    5. Win the race. First rider to reach Parliament Square wins BODA BLITZ!

    Tip: on a phone, play in landscape or scroll the board area — everything still works.

    Game Rules

    SquareEffect
    🚧 Traffic JamLose your next turn
    🕳 PotholeMove back 2 spaces
    ⛽ Fuel StationMove forward 2 spaces
    🚔 Police CheckPay 2 coins or lose your next turn
    🛒 MarketCollect 2 coins
    🟢 Safe LaneNo effect (draws a bonus card if enabled)
    ⚡ ShortcutMove forward 3 spaces
    🔄 City RoundaboutRoll again
    🏍 Boda BoostMove forward 1 space
    • All riders start at position 0 (Owino Market).
    • You must land exactly on FINISH (position 41).
    • Special squares never chain: a square reached by a special effect does nothing.
    • Coins are game points only — they are not real money.

    About

    BODA BLITZ — Race Through Kampala! is a free browser board game inspired by Kampala's boda boda culture, its markets, its potholes and its traffic.

    Built with HTML5, CSS3 and vanilla JavaScript. A Python/Flask backend is included for future online multiplayer.

    Coins in this game are virtual points. No real money is involved.
